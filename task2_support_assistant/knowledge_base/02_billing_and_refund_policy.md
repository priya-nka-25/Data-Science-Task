# Subscription Billing and Refund Policy

## Section 2.1: Subscription Billing Cycles & Payment Terms
Subscriptions are billed either on a monthly recurring basis or an annual prepaid cycle. Monthly plans are invoiced on the 1st of each calendar month and automatically charged to the primary payment method. Annual contracts offer a 20% discount and include net-30 invoicing terms for enterprise clients with credit approval. All payments must be settled in USD, EUR, or GBP.

## Section 2.2: Refund Eligibility and Pro-Rated Calculations
Refunds are strictly governed by account tier and billing duration:
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: First-time annual subscribers may request a 100% full refund within 30 days of initial purchase, no questions asked.
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellations**: Monthly recurring subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins, but access remains active until the end of the paid month.
- **Annual Pro-Rated Refunds**: If an annual contract is cancelled mid-term due to documented service-level breaches (uptime drops below 99.5%), a pro-rated refund is calculated based on remaining whole months minus applicable discounts.

## Section 2.3: Grace Periods and Failed Payment Handling
If a recurring payment fails, accounts enter a 14-day grace period. Automated retry attempts are scheduled for Day 3, Day 7, and Day 12. If unresolved by Day 14, the account is throttled to read-only access. Full account suspension occurs at Day 30 without payment.
