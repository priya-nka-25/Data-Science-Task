# Enterprise SLA & Support Tiers Policy

## Section 1.1: Support Tiers Overview
We offer three distinct support tiers designed for varying operational requirements: Standard, Business, and Enterprise Dedicated. Standard support covers basic operational guidance and ticket-based assistance during normal business hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday). Business tier introduces extended 18/5 coverage and live chat access. Enterprise Dedicated provides 24/7/365 multi-channel support including dedicated Technical Account Managers (TAMs) and direct Slack/Teams bridging.

## Section 1.2: Response Time Commitments (SLA)
First response time targets are strictly prioritized based on incident severity:
- **Severity 1 (Critical / Outage)**: System completely unavailable or critical business workflows halted. Response time is under 15 minutes for Enterprise, 1 hour for Business, and 4 hours for Standard.
- **Severity 2 (High / Degraded)**: Major feature impairment with workaround available. Response time is within 1 hour for Enterprise, 4 hours for Business, and 8 business hours for Standard.
- **Severity 3 (Medium / Normal)**: Non-critical bugs or minor operational degradation. Response time target is within 4 hours for Enterprise, 12 hours for Business, and 24 hours for Standard.
- **Severity 4 (Low / General Inquiry)**: General questions, documentation queries, or feature suggestions. Response time is within 12 hours for Enterprise and 48 hours for Standard.

## Section 1.3: Incident Escalation Matrix
When a Severity 1 incident exceeds 30 minutes without resolution or a verified mitigation plan, the incident automatically escalates to the VP of Engineering and the Duty Incident Commander. If resolution exceeds 2 hours, the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) is briefed and an hourly customer status advisory is broadcast on the external status portal.
